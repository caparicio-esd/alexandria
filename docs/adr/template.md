# NNNN. Title, as a decision in the imperative

Status: Proposed
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces. What was true when this came up, what constraints were fixed, and
what made a decision necessary at all. Written so somebody who was not there can
tell why the obvious answer was not obviously right.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense and as few sentences as it takes.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it makes easy, and what it makes hard. The honest half —
a record with no downsides listed is a record that was not thought through.

## What would change this

The conditions under which the decision should be revisited. If there are none,
say so.
